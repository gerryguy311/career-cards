How your cards were picked
  1. Your roles become colors. Every Magic color is a practitioner style: White is structure (GRC, privacy, audit), Blue is knowledge (intel, engineering, architecture), Black is ambition (offensive security), Red is speed (SOC, incident response) and Green is growth (sysadmin, cloud, data, infrastructure). Your work style and the skills you typed in break any ties.
  2. Each card adds a color. Careers grow by adding skills, not replacing them, so each stage keeps your colors and adds at least one new one. Your very first color stays with you the whole way, like a commander in Magic.
  3. Cards come from real Magic. We search for popular legendary creatures with exactly your colors, so many players will recognize them. Don’t love one? Redraw it.
  4. AI tells the story. The “why this card” lines and the summary up top are written by AI from the card’s abilities and your answers.

Don’t love a card? Tap “Redraw” under it for another legend in the same colors. The “why this card” lines are written by AI, so take them with a grain of salt.

How it works

Every Magic color is a practitioner style. White brings structure (GRC, policy). Blue brings knowledge (intel, architecture). Black brings ambition (offensive security). Red brings speed (SOC, incident response). Green brings growth (infrastructure, cloud).

Your first card is the color you start in. Each step adds a color, and your first color stays with you as your commander. Find your color. Then find your next one.

From the talk Untap, Upkeep, Level Up: What Magic: The Gathering Teaches Us About Crushing a Cyber Career by Gerald Auger, PhD.
